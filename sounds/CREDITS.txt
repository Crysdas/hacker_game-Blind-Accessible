Sound credits
=============

Sound effects (terminal clicks, blips, menus, alarms, lasers, explosions,
access granted/denied, warnings):
  "The Essential Retro Video Game Sound Effects Collection [512 sounds]"
  by Juhani Junkala (SubspaceAudio)
  Source: OpenGameArt - https://opengameart.org/content/512-sound-effects-8-bit-style
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal - https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Keyboard sounds (terminal/click/type/beep - real mechanical keyboard
  recordings, Cherry KC1000 through a Shure SM7B):
  "Keyboard Soundpack #1 [Typing and Single Keystrokes]" by unicaegames
  Source: OpenGameArt - https://opengameart.org/content/keyboard-soundpack-1-typing-and-single-keystrokes
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal

Synthesized tone files (tones/tone_*.wav, tones/tone_short_*.wav):
  Generated sine waves, public domain.

Background music:
  "Битва с Тенью" (battle_with_shadow.mp3) - provided by the project author.

Ambient hum (ambient/computer_hum.ogg):
  Kenney assets (CC0) - https://kenney.nl

Logo intro (sounds/ui/logo_riser.ogg, logo_hit.ogg, logo_chime.ogg,
logo_sparkle.ogg):
  Synthesized with ffmpeg (pink-noise riser, low-frequency impact, bell
  chime, sparkle arpeggio), stereo, public domain.
